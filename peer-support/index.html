Peer Support & Mentorship Safety

This notice is not published yet. It is acknowledged in the app before a first mentor request, but the standalone page has not been written or approved. Until it is, the points it covers are set out in the documents below.

What you should know about peer support at Hera:

  • Mentors are volunteers sharing lived experience — not healthcare professionals acting in that capacity.
  • They cannot diagnose, treat, or give medical advice, and they are not trained to respond to a crisis.
  • Hera is not an emergency service. In an emergency, contact emergency services first.
  • Share only what you are comfortable sharing.
  • If anything feels wrong, report it to Hera from the Mentor tab.

These points are stated in full in Terms of Use, section 8 and in the Mentor Agreement.

Hera is an unincorporated, student-led project in Singapore providing non-clinical peer support. It is not a medical or emergency service.
