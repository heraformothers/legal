# Hera — Privacy Notice

**Version 2026-10-07.1 · Effective [PUBLICATION DATE]**

Hera is an unincorporated, student-led project based in Singapore that provides non-clinical peer support for mothers. Hera is not a registered company, a clinic, or a healthcare provider. "We" means the **Hera Project Team** — the Hera team — which is responsible for Hera and for the decisions described in this notice.

Our designated data-protection contact is **Hera Data Protection Lead**, at **heraformothers@gmail.com**. You can use that address for any privacy question, or to ask for access to or correction of your information — see section 11.

This notice covers the Hera iOS app and the account behind it. It does not cover the independent organisations whose helpline numbers we list.

## 2. Information collected automatically

Very little.

- **App version**, and a basic device-compatibility signal, stored only alongside your consent records.
- **A rate-limiting counter.** When you type your email address at the first step of sign-up, we check whether it is already registered so you are not asked to fill in seven steps first — and, if you are signing up as a peer mentor, whether that address was invited to be one. To stop that check being abused to discover who has an account, we keep a counter against a **scrambled (hashed) form of the network address** the request came from. The address itself is never stored, and the counter is not connected to your account.

Some information is kept **on your own phone**: a local copy of your profile and journal entries (which are also stored on our servers, as described above), your in-app preferences, and — if you begin sign-up and do not finish — a saved draft of your answers, including the email address you typed. The sign-up draft and those preferences are never sent to us. Deleting the app removes all of it from the phone.

We do **not** collect: IP addresses, device fingerprints, advertising identifiers, analytics events, crash reports, contacts, or your location, and Hera does not use your camera. Hera opens your photo library or microphone only when you choose a photo or file to send or to use as your profile photo, or record a voice note — and only that photo, file or recording reaches us (section 1). There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no AI or large-language-model service in the app.

**Reminders are scheduled on your phone**, not sent from our servers, so this version does not collect or store a push-notification token. If we later add reminders sent from our servers, we will update this notice first. Any reminder we ever send will use generic wording — it will never include journal text, a mood value, pregnancy information, report details, or anything else sensitive on your lock screen.

Our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Expo, Apple) may log IP addresses and technical request information at their own platform level as part of delivering the service. That logging is theirs, not ours, and we do not receive it as a feed of your activity.

## 3. Sensitive information

Some of what you put into Hera is health-related or close to it: your mood check-ins, your journal, anything you disclose in Circle or in a mentor request, and your pregnancy or postpartum stage. We treat all of it as sensitive.

**Your journal and mood check-ins are private to you.** They are stored on our servers under access rules that permit only your own account to read them. They are not shown to other members, to mentors, or to moderators, and they are never used for matching. Section 8 explains the one limit on this: the people who administer our database have technical access to what is stored there.

**If you write something that suggests you may be at risk**, Hera may show you a supportive message and a link to crisis resources. This check happens entirely on your device, by looking at the words you typed before they are saved. **No alert is sent to any person** — not to a mentor, a moderator, an administrator, or an emergency service. Hera does not monitor you and no one is watching for signs that you are in danger.

**Hera does not offer a wellbeing questionnaire, self-check or screening tool in this version**, and collects no answers, scores or results of that kind. If we introduce optional wellbeing tools in future, we will update this notice and the Terms first, and ask you to read them.

**Information about your baby and other people.** Some of what you tell us is about someone else — your baby's age, your children's ages, or a partner or family member you mention in a post, a journal entry or a mentor request. We protect it the same way we protect everything else in this notice. Please share only what you need to, especially about people who cannot speak for themselves.

## 4. Your name in Circle

In Circle, your posts and replies show your **display name** by default. For any single post or reply you can choose **"Use my Circle nickname"** ("Use Circle nickname" on a reply), and other members then see your **Circle nickname** instead — a nickname you choose, which cannot be your display name. The choice applies to that post or reply only and is not remembered. The composer tells you which name will be shown before you post. Your profile photo is never shown in Circle.

The name a post or reply is published under is recorded when you publish it and does not change afterwards — not if you later change your nickname, and not if you change your display name. Content published before this version keeps exactly the label it already had: posts and replies shown under your Circle name continue to show it, and content you posted anonymously continues to show "Anonymous mother".

Under a nickname, your display name is not sent to other members' devices at all, so posting under a nickname is pseudonymous to other members. It is not anonymous to Hera: every post and reply — under a nickname, your name, or anonymous — is still linked to your account internally, so that we can act on safety reports and enforce the Terms. Moderators and administrators can identify its author when they need to.

## 5. How we use your information

- To create, secure, and operate your account
- To recognise an address we invited to be a peer mentor, and to grant that account the mentor role when it signs up or next signs in
- To show a mentor's profile — the name she chose, what she has written about herself, what she can help with, and the languages she speaks — to mothers looking for support
- To deliver messages, photos, files and voice notes between a matched mentor and mentee, and to show each of you when the other has read them
- To keep a history of your in-app notifications
- To show your posts and comments to the community under the name each was published under — your display name, or your Circle nickname where you chose it — and to show you a list of your own posts
- To keep your journal and mood check-ins private to you
- To recommend mentor matches and to run mentorship once both sides agree
- To look into reports and moderate content, which includes identifying the author of content posted under a nickname or anonymously where that is necessary for safety
- To send the reminders you have turned on
- To keep a record of the legal documents you have accepted
- To meet legal obligations

We do **not** use your information for advertising, for profiling unrelated to mentor matching, for academic research or publication, or to train AI systems, and we do not sell it. If any of that ever changes, it will require a separate review, an updated notice, and — where consent is the right basis — a separate opt-in that you can decline without losing access to Hera.

## 8. How we protect your information

- Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
- Our database enforces, row by row, that your private content is readable only by your own account.
- Administrative privileges cannot be granted by the app itself; they can only be set by an administrator directly in the database.
- You can lock your journal behind Face ID on your device. This protects the journal screen in the app; it does not separately encrypt what is stored, and it is skipped if your phone has no biometric set up.
- Passwords are handled by our authentication provider; Hera's own code never receives them.
- Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
- **Messages, photos, files and voice notes between mentors and mentees are not end-to-end encrypted.** They are protected in transit and at rest, but they are readable by our infrastructure in principle, and we can access them if you report a conversation.

**Who can reach your information.** It is worth being precise about this, because "private" can mean several different things:

- **Other members** see only what you choose to publish, under your name or the nickname you chose, and — if you are matched with them — the messages you send them. They never see your journal, your mood check-ins, your email address, your reports, or the display name behind a nickname or an anonymous post.
- **In-app moderators** can review, hide and remove Circle content and can identify the author of anonymous content. Moderator tools give **no** access to journal entries or mood check-ins.
- **Project administrators** are a small number of named people on the Hera team who administer the database and hosting account. Journal entries and mood check-ins are protected by account-level database access controls and are not available through ordinary moderator functions. However, a limited number of authorised project administrators with infrastructure-level access **may technically access information** through our hosting and database tools where reasonably necessary for security, maintenance, support, legal compliance or incident response. They do not routinely read private journal entries or mood check-ins, and casual access is prohibited.
- **Our infrastructure providers** hold the data on their systems and can technically access it as part of running those systems, subject to their own terms and security commitments.

We keep the number of administrators to the minimum we need, each uses their own individual account with multi-factor authentication and no shared credentials, and administrative access is logged where our providers make logging available.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that your information will never be accessed without authorisation. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will act on it and notify you and the Personal Data Protection Commission where the law requires.

## 9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile. It is permanent and cannot be undone. This is a real deletion, not a hidden or deactivated account.

**What is removed immediately** from our production database: your login, profile, motherhood details, mood check-ins, journal entries, Circle posts and comments, reactions, saved posts, topic suggestions and votes, members you blocked and blocks others placed on you, mentor and mentee profiles, mentor matching preferences, mentorship requests and connections, your profile photo, notification settings and notification history, and consent records.

**Conversations are deleted whole.** A conversation belongs to both people in it, so deleting your account deletes every mentorship conversation you are part of — your messages and the other person's, the photos, files and voice notes either of you sent, and the archived text of edited or deleted messages — for both of you. The same happens to your conversation if the other person deletes her account. Hera's own data on your phone — the local copies of your profile, journal, preferences and blocked members — is cleared at the same time.

**What takes a little longer.** Our database provider keeps short-term internal copies of the database so the service can be restored after a failure. We cannot browse, search, or restore individual records from those copies, and we never use them to look anything up. Deleted information disappears from them as they age out on the provider's normal cycle.

**What is kept.** Reports and safety records connected to you are retained as described in section 10.

**Deleting a single post.** When you delete one of your Circle posts, it is deleted from our database together with every reply to it — it is not hidden or kept.

**What we cannot remove.** Anything another member has already seen, copied, quoted or screenshotted is outside our control, and deleting your account does not reach it. If you begin sign-up but never finish, the draft saved on your phone is removed when you delete the app. Anything you submitted through the Google-hosted mentor application form sits outside this process — email us and we will delete it.

## 10. Reports and safety records

You report a post, a comment, a message or a member by emailing us at heraformothers@gmail.com. We keep your email and a record of what we did about it. We need them to act on the report, to notice repeated behaviour, and to be able to explain a decision later.

Reports are read only by the Hera team. They are never shown to the member you reported and never to other members. Earlier versions of Hera let you file reports inside the app; those are kept under the same rules.

We keep a report for up to **12 months** after it is resolved, and then delete it or irreversibly de-identify it. We may keep one for longer only where a complaint is still unresolved, where there is an ongoing safety or security incident, or where we are required to keep it by law.

If you delete your account, reports filed in the app that are connected to you are kept for the remainder of that period **with your identity removed**, so that a safety record survives without pointing back at you. Reports you emailed us are not removed by account deletion; ask us and we will delete them, unless the report is still open or we must keep it for one of the reasons above.

Records of the legal documents you accepted are kept while your account exists and are deleted with it. This means we do not retain evidence of what a departed user agreed to — we have chosen to prioritise deletion over that evidence.

## 11. Your choices and your rights

- **Access.** You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you. Email the address in section 13. There is no self-service export in this version, so we will put it together by hand.
- **Correction.** You can edit your name, Circle nickname and profile photo in Profile, and your support preferences in the mentor survey, at any time. You can ask us to correct anything you cannot edit yourself.
- **Withdrawing consent.** You can withdraw your consent to Hera's collection and use of your information at any time by deleting your account. Because Hera cannot provide an account, a community or mentorship without it, withdrawing means your account and its data are deleted — we will make that clear before you confirm.
- **Notifications.** Reminders are optional and separate from accepting these documents. You can turn them off in Settings or in iOS at any time, and declining them does not restrict any other part of Hera.
- **Blocking.** You can block another member at any time from a post menu or from a conversation's menu, and unblock them later under Profile → Blocked members.
- **Complaints.** If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, tell us first — we would rather fix it. You also have the right to complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission.

We aim to respond to any request within 14 days.

### Optional matching answers {#matching}

Earlier versions of the mentor survey asked, optionally, for your age range, employment status, marital status and the ages of your children, and for your preferred way of keeping in touch. This version does not ask for them and does not use them. In October 2026 we deleted the answers anyone had given, keeping only the age of your youngest child, which you still give in the survey and which a peer helper sees on your request.
