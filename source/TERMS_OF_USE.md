## 3. What Hera is

Hera offers **non-clinical peer support**. It is a place to write privately, track how you are feeling, talk with other mothers, and be matched with a peer mentor.

**Hera is not a medical service.** It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, clinical assessment, screening, or medical advice, and using it does not create a doctor–patient, therapist–client, counsellor–client, or any other professional care relationship between you and Hera or between you and anyone you meet through Hera. Hera has not been licensed or approved by any medical governing body.

**Hera is not an emergency service.** It cannot provide an emergency response. No one at Hera is monitoring the app for people in danger.

Nothing in Hera is a substitute for care from a qualified health professional. If you have concerns about your health or your baby's health, speak to a doctor, midwife, or other qualified professional.

The mood check-in, the journal and the gentle exercises in the Care tab are **supportive tools, not measurements or assessments**. They produce no score, no result and no clinical meaning of any kind. Hera may introduce optional wellbeing tools in a future version, but only after these Terms and the Privacy Notice have been updated to describe them.

## 4. Support listed

**If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact emergency services directly. Do not rely on Hera.**

Hera lists crisis helpline numbers for Singapore. Tapping one opens your phone's dialler and connects you directly to that service — Hera is not part of the call and does not record it.

These numbers are stored in the app and are not updated automatically. They may become out of date, and the organisations behind them are independent of Hera. We do not control, endorse, or take responsibility for the services they provide.

If you write something in Hera that suggests you may be at risk, the app may show you a message and a link to these resources. This check happens entirely on your own device. **This does not alert anyone.** No mentor, moderator, administrator, or emergency service is notified, automatically or otherwise.

## 5. The Circle community

The Circle is a shared space. When you post there, you agree to:

- **Be respectful.** Treat other members with kindness.
- **No hate.** No abuse, harassment, slurs, or attacks on any person or group.
- **No medical advice.** Share your experience; do not tell other members what to do about their health, medication, or their baby's health.
- **No solicitation.** No selling, advertising, recruiting, or promotion.
- **Report problems.** Email the Hera team rather than confronting a member yourself — Report in the app shows the address.

You also agree not to: impersonate anyone; post another person's private information; post content that is unlawful, sexually explicit, or graphically violent; post content that encourages self-harm, suicide, or disordered eating; scrape or republish other members' posts; or use Hera to disrupt or attack the service.

The full community rules are set out in our **Community Guidelines**, which you accept before your first post or comment.

Use the trigger-warning option when your post covers material which may be difficult for others.

Talking honestly about how hard motherhood is — including distress, low mood, intrusive thoughts and asking for help — is exactly what the Circle is for. These rules are not intended to stop you saying how you actually feel.

In Circle, your posts and replies show your **display name** by default. For any post or reply you can choose **"Use Circle nickname"**, and it shows your **Circle nickname** instead — a nickname you choose, which cannot be your display name. The choice applies to that post or reply only. Your profile photo is never shown in Circle. Each post and reply keeps the name it was published under, even if you later change your nickname or display name, and content published before this version keeps the label it already had.

**A nickname only changes what other members see.** Posting under a nickname is pseudonymous to other members, not anonymous to Hera. Every post and reply — under a nickname, your name, or anonymous — remains linked internally to your account, and our moderators and administrators can identify you as the author. We keep this link so we can respond to safety reports and enforce these Terms. Post accordingly.

**Reporting and blocking.** To report a post, a comment, a message or a member, email the Hera team at **heraformothers@gmail.com** — the Report option in a post's menu or a conversation's menu shows the address. Hera has no in-app report form and sends nothing on your behalf. You can block another member in the app. Blocking hides that member's posts and comments from you and hides yours from them, and it prevents a mentorship connection between you. Reports go to the Hera team; they are not shown to the member you reported, and other members cannot see them.

## 8. Mentorship

**Mentors are ordinary Hera members who volunteered.** They are not our employees, agents, contractors, or representatives, and they are not acting as healthcare professionals. Being listed as a mentor is not a professional qualification, an endorsement, or a statement that Hera has verified anyone's background or training.

Mentors offer peer support based on their own experience. They cannot give you medical advice and are not permitted to. They are not trained to respond to a crisis and are under no obligation to be available or to respond.

Mentors agree to a separate **Mentor Confidentiality, Boundaries and Conduct Agreement** before they can be listed.

**What mentorship is in this version.** You can see approved mentors who are free to take someone on, send a mentor a request with a short introduction, and a mentor can accept or decline it. A mentor who declines by mistake can take it back within a few seconds; the request then waits for her answer again. Where a request is accepted, the two of you can message each other privately in Hera.

**One mentor at a time.** You have one mentor at a time. A mentor chooses how many mothers she can support at once, up to ten; once she has reached that number she does not appear to anyone else looking for support until a place frees up. When a mentorship ends, you are free to request a mentor again, or either of you can ask to reconnect; reconnecting resumes the same conversation.

**In the app, messages between a matched mentor and mentee — including photos, files and voice notes — can be read only by the two of you.**

They are encrypted in transit and at rest, but they are **not end-to-end encrypted**. We do not routinely monitor or read them. A small number of authorised administrators can technically access stored messages through our hosting infrastructure, and do so only where reasonably necessary — to look into a conversation you report to us, or for security, maintenance, legal compliance or incident response. The other person can screenshot, copy, or repeat anything you send.

You can edit or delete a message you sent for 15 minutes after sending it. The other person will see that it was edited or deleted, and we keep the original text in a restricted archive so that a report can still be looked into (see the Privacy Notice). Deleting your account deletes your conversations from our servers — both people's messages and files — for both of you.

Either you or your mentor can end a mentorship, which stops new messages; the conversation history remains visible to both of you.

Hera never discloses your phone number, email address or other contact details to another member, and members must not ask each other for them. Communication outside Hera is not supported or permitted in this version. If you choose to contact another member outside Hera anyway, you do so entirely at your own risk: none of Hera's protections — reporting, blocking, moderation, or any record of what was said — apply there.

We may end, suspend, or decline a mentorship at any time. Either you or your mentor can end a connection at any time.

You are choosing to connect with a stranger. Use your judgement, and share only what you are comfortable sharing.
