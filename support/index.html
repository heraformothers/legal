Support

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact emergency services directly. Do not rely on Hera. In Singapore: 995 for an ambulance, 999 for the police.

Hera is an unincorporated, student-led project providing non-clinical peer support for pregnant and postpartum mothers in Singapore. Hera is not a medical service and not an emergency service. No one at Hera is monitoring the app for people in danger.

Contact us

To report something, or for help with the app, a privacy request, or a complaint, email heraformothers@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 14 days.
