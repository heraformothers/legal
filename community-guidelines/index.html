These guidelines apply to the Circle. You accept them before your first post or comment. They expand on section 5 of the Terms of Use; where the two differ, the Terms apply.

Kept as a separate document because it is gated separately in the app (you accept it at your first post, not at sign-up), it is versioned separately, and it is the document a member is most likely to be pointed back to. Folding it into the Terms would make the moment of acceptance meaningless.

The five rules

  1. Be respectful. Treat other members with kindness.
  2. No hate. No abuse, harassment, slurs, or attacks on any person or group.
  3. No medical advice. Share your experience; do not tell other members what to do about their health, medication, or their baby's health.
  4. No solicitation. No selling, advertising, recruiting, or promotion.
  5. Report problems. Use the report function rather than confronting a member yourself.

Also not allowed

Impersonating anyone. Posting another person's private information. Content that is unlawful, sexually explicit, or graphically violent. Content that encourages self-harm, suicide, or disordered eating. Scraping or republishing other members' posts. Using Hera to disrupt or attack the service.

What is allowed, and wanted

Saying how hard it is. Anger, resentment, numbness, regret, intrusive thoughts, not feeling bonded, not enjoying it, wanting your old life back. Asking for help. Saying you are not coping.

None of that breaks these rules. The Circle exists for it. Please do not soften what you are going through because you think it is too much for this space.

There is a difference between saying you are struggling — always welcome — and encouraging someone else to hurt themselves, which is not.

Anonymity

You can post anonymously, and comments are always anonymous. Other members see only "Anonymous mother".

Anonymity is limited to what other members see. Every anonymous post stays linked to your account internally, and Hera's moderators can identify you as the author. We keep that link so we can act on safety reports and enforce these rules. Post accordingly.

Trigger warnings

Use the trigger-warning option when your post covers something another mother may not be ready to read — loss, birth trauma, self-harm, feeding difficulties, violence. Warned posts are hidden behind a tap.

Reporting and blocking

Report any post or comment from the post menu. Reports go to the Hera team. They are never shown to the member you reported, and other members cannot see them. Reporting someone in good faith will never count against you.

Block a member from the same menu. Blocking hides their posts and comments from you and yours from them, and prevents a mentorship connection between you. You can block someone whose posts are anonymous — that blocks the person, not everyone posting anonymously.

What happens when a rule is broken

We check reports at least once a day during this initial rollout and aim to review one within 24 hours. That is a target, not a guarantee, and no one is watching the Circle in real time.

We may hide or remove content, and we may restrict, suspend or terminate an account. We may identify the author of anonymous content when doing so. Where it is practical and safe, we will tell you what we did.

Some posts are also checked automatically before they appear. Those checks are simple: they miss things, and they sometimes hold something harmless. If yours was held in error, tell us.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call emergency services — 995 for an ambulance, 999 for the police. Do not rely on a report, on Hera, or on another member.
